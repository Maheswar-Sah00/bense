# Bense: how common and costly box-dye failure is (sourced numbers)

Research date: 2026-10-16. Web research only.

**How to read this file**
- Each item lists the number, what it measures, the source, the date, the URL, a short exact quote, the region, and a quality label.
- Quality labels:
  - **[DATA]**: government, peer-reviewed or independent research firm.
  - **[BRAND SURVEY]**: commissioned by a company that sells hair colour or salon services. These are real polls but have a commercial motive.
  - **[MARKET REPORT]**: summary of a paid analyst report. Sizes differ a lot between firms.
  - **[NEWS]**: a news outlet reporting a case or trend.
  - **[WEAK SOURCE]**: blog, listing site, salon marketing, or a figure I could only see second-hand.
- **"Verified"** means I opened the page and the quote was there. **"Not opened"** means the number came from a search-index summary because the page blocked fetching. Re-check those before putting them on a slide.
- Nothing here is invented. Where I found no data (for example, a US ER-visit count for hair dye, or box-return rates), the file says so.

## 4. Physical harm

**4.1 NHS: "Hair dye reactions are fairly common but are usually mild"** [DATA, government], UK
- Source: NHS, "Hair dye reactions". Last reviewed 29 Dec 2025.
- URL: https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/hair-dye-reactions/
- Quotes: "Hair dye reactions are fairly common but are usually mild."; "Symptoms can take up to 72 hours to appear."; "It's possible to have a serious allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) to hair dye, but this is rare." Verified.

**4.2 FDA: hair dyes can cause allergic reactions, hair loss, and blindness near the eyes; coal-tar dyes need no FDA approval** [DATA, government], US
- Source: US FDA, "Hair Dyes". Content current as of 25 Sep 2025.
- URL: https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/hair-dyes
- Quotes: "Some coal-tar hair dyes can cause allergic reactions or sensitization that may result in skin irritation and hair loss."; "Hair dyes have caused eye injuries, including blindness, when used in the eye area."; "Coal-tar hair dyes, unlike color additives in general, do not need FDA approval." Verified.

**4.3 India: 42% of hair-dye users reported adverse reactions; 34% never did a skin test** [DATA, peer-reviewed], India
- Source: Patel et al., *Int J Trichology* 2013 (see 1.13). 263 respondents.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24574693/
- Quote: "Skin test was never performed in 34% prior to their use. … Adverse reactions were reported by 42%, with headache (63%) and itching (38%) being the most common." Verified.
- Also: "They continued dyeing despite adverse reactions, which indicates cosmetic importance." Verified.

**4.4 India: 11.5% of dermatitis patients patch-test positive to PPD, versus 6% in North America, 4.4% in Asia, 4.1% in Europe** [DATA, peer-reviewed review], India / global
- Source: Palaniappan V, Karthikeyan K, Anusuya S. "Dermatological adverse effects of hair dye use: A narrative review." *Indian J Dermatol Venereol Leprol* 2024;90:458-70.
- URL: https://ijdvl.com/dermatological-adverse-effects-of-hair-dye-use-a-narrative-review/
- Quote: positive patch tests "amounting to 11.5% in India". The review also says PPD is "found in nearly 70% of the hair dyes available worldwide". Verified.
- Caveats: the 11.5% traces back to a single earlier Indian study. These are dermatitis-clinic patients, not the general population.

**4.5 Europe: 4.6% of 21,515 dermatitis patients were PPD-allergic; home hair dyeing was the top cause (41.8%)** [DATA, peer-reviewed], Europe incl. UK
- Source: Thyssen JP et al. *Contact Dermatitis* 2009;60(6):314-9. 10 centres, 2003–2007.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19489965/
- Quote: "The weighted average prevalence was 4.6% among 21 515 patients … Consumer hair dyeing was the most prominent cause of PPD sensitization (weighted average 41.8%)." Verified via PubMed.

**4.6 General population: 1.3% PPD-sensitized (Netherlands)** [DATA, peer-reviewed], Europe
- Source: Vogel TA et al. *Contact Dermatitis* 2015;72(6):355-61.
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25824090/
- Quote: "PPD sensitization occurred in 3.3% of the patch tested population and in 1.3% of the general population." Verified.
- Conservative extrapolation: if about 1% of adults are PPD-sensitized, that is millions of people per country who could react to a standard dark box dye. This is an inference, not a measured number.

**4.7 UK: London clinic saw PPD-positive patch tests double in six years, to 7.1%** [DATA, old], UK
- Source: BMJ editorial, reported by ScienceDaily, "Allergy To Hair Dye Increasing", 5 Feb 2007.
- URL: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2007/02/070205110939.htm
- Quotes: "More than two thirds of hair dyes currently contain para-phenylenediamine (PPD)"; "a doubling in frequency over six years to 7.1%". Verified.
- A newer London series (2014–2023) found 114 of 3,958 patients (2.9%) PPD-positive. That figure is from a *Br J Dermatol* 2026 abstract seen via search, **not opened**.

**4.8 UAE: 73% of hair-dye users never patch-test** [DATA, peer-reviewed], global comparison
- Source: Turkish J Pharm Sci, Dec 2024. 333 respondents, northern Emirates, data collected Feb–Jul 2022.
- URL: https://turkjps.org/articles/general-public-knowledge-attitudes-and-practice-towards-hair-dye-usage-and-its-adverse-effects-in-the-northern-emirates-of-the-united-arab-emirates/doi/tjps.galenos.2024.33489
- Quotes: "72.97% answered that they had never previously performed an allergic test before using HD"; "only 8.4% responded that they had experienced allergic reactions". Verified.

**4.9 A new hair-dye allergen named "Allergen of the Year 2025"** [DATA, professional society], US
- Source: Atwater AR, Botto N. "Toluene-2,5-Diamine Sulfate: Allergen of the Year 2025." *Dermatitis* 2025;36(1):3-11 (American Contact Dermatitis Society).
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39862176/
- Quote: PTDS is "often omitted from standard patch test screening series, possibly leading to underdiagnosis." Verified.

**4.10 US emergency departments: hair-care products caused 52.4% of hospitalised cosmetic injuries in under-5s (2002–2016)** [DATA, NEISS], US
- Source: Nationwide Children's Hospital / *Clinical Pediatrics*, 17 Jun 2019. Based on CPSC NEISS data.
- URL: https://www.nationwidechildrens.org/newsroom/news-releases/2019/06/cosmetics-injuries-study
- Quotes: "64,686 children younger than five years of age were treated in U.S. emergency departments" for cosmetic injuries. Hair care was 27.0% of injuries, and among hospitalisations "more than half were from hair care products (52.4%)". Verified.
- Caveat: these are children, and the hair-care category is mostly relaxers and perms. It is **not** a count of adult box-dye injuries. **Gap:** I found no published NEISS estimate of adult hair-dye ER visits. CPSC's NEISS query tool (https://www.cpsc.gov/Research--Statistics/NEISS-Injury-Data) lets you search case narratives for "hair dye" to produce one.

**4.11 Scalp chemical burns from bleach: 71% full-thickness, 6 of 14 needed skin grafts** [DATA, peer-reviewed], Australia
- Source: Noori O et al. "Chemical burns to scalp from hair bleach: a retrospective case series." *Australasian J Plastic Surgery* 2024;7(2). Published 18 Jun 2024. Two Sydney burns units, 2016–2020.
- URL: https://ajops.com/article/88914-chemical-burns-to-scalp-from-hair-bleach-a-retrospective-case-series
- Quotes: "Ten patients had full thickness components to their burns (n = 10, 71%)"; "One patient received adequate first aid at the time of injury." Verified.
- Caveat: 71% of these cases happened in salons, not at home.

- Source: Consumer Reports, "Some Boxed Hair Dyes Contain Fewer Toxins Than Others" by Leigh-Ann Jackson, 14 Apr 2026.

## 5. Emotional impact

**5.1 Yale (2000): remembering a "bad hair day" measurably lowered self-esteem; men were hit harder on feeling "less smart"** [DATA, academic, P&G-funded], US
- Source: Marianne LaFrance, Yale University. 60 men and 60 women, mostly students. Reported by AP / Deseret News, 27 Jan 2000.
- URL: https://www.deseret.com/2000/1/27/19487744/bad-hair-days-affect-men-too-study-finds/
- Finding: people who recalled bad-hair days scored lower on self-esteem and felt less smart, less capable, more embarrassed and less sociable. LaFrance: "This is not just the domain of women." Verified.
- Caveats: small convenience sample, funded by Procter & Gamble (a shampoo maker).

**5.2 UK: 1 in 5 women have cried over a bad hair day; 6% cancelled plans; 9% hid it under a hat or scarf** [BRAND SURVEY], UK
- Source: OGX "Hairstories" survey of 2,000 UK women. HJI, 22 Jan 2024.
- URL: https://hji.co.uk/61-feel-empowered-when-our-hair-is-looking-great
- Quotes: "one in five women (20%) have been reduced to tears as a result of a bad hair day"; "6% even canceled plans as they couldn't bear the thought of friends and family seeing them". Verified.

**5.3 US: boosting confidence and mood is the top reason women colour their hair (53%)** [BRAND SURVEY], US
- Source: Better Natured, Mar 2024 (URL in 1.7).
- Quote: "boosting confidence and mood is the leading motivator for over half of women who use hair color (53%)". Verified.
- Pitch use: people colour to feel better, so a failed colour delivers the opposite of what they paid for.

**5.4 UK: 67% say trying new hairstyles was a big part of finding their identity; 51% have tried up to six colours** [BRAND SURVEY], UK
- Source: OGX survey, Jan 2024 (URL in 5.2). Verified.

**5.5 Real cases, multi-day distress (news)** [NEWS]
- Newsweek, 8 Nov 2024. A 19-year-old used a Garnier Olia 3.0 box dye with no patch test, needed two hospital trips and 20 days of steroids, and said "never again". https://www.newsweek.com/woman-suffers-allergic-reaction-hair-dye-1980509 (verified)
- Newsweek, 26 Oct 2023, "I Dyed My Hair Black and It Nearly Killed Me". 10 days in hospital; her TikTok drew about half a million views in a few days. https://www.newsweek.com/woman-almost-dies-allergic-reaction-hair-dye-1837829 (verified)
- These match the founder's story ("upset for days"). Use them as illustrations, not as statistics.

**5.6 Gap:** I found no peer-reviewed study that measures the emotional impact of a *colour* failure specifically, such as days of distress or missed work. All the figures above concern "bad hair" in general or come from brand polls.

## 8. Returns, waste, repeat purchases

- **No data found** on unused or abandoned dye boxes, retailer return rates for hair colour, or "bought a second box to fix the first". Retailers and brands do not publish this. It is a gap, and Bense could measure it itself (for example, with an in-app question: "Have you ever bought a second box to fix the first?").

  - Frequency: 68% of home dyers re-colour at least every five months (Better Natured 2024, verified). In the UK, "57% of men who colour their hair do so once a month or more" (Mintel 2018, verified).
  - Fix-it products sell: Color Oops colour remover reportedly +90% in 2020 (Chain Drug Review, **not opened**).
  - India: "Over half of former hair colour users agree that there is a lack of haircare products to look after coloured hair" (Mintel India 2024, verified). This points to people dropping out of the category after bad experiences.

## Top 10 headline numbers for a pitch

Ranked by credibility first, then punch. Each line is pitch-ready wording, followed by its source tag.

1. **45% of American women have coloured their hair to cover grey.** Pew Research Center, survey of 8,750 adults, Nov 2025. [DATA; verify on pewresearch.org, page was not opened]
2. **More than half of UK women (53%) colour their hair at home.** Mintel, 2018 (2,000 adults). [DATA]
3. **Hair dye allergy is "fairly common" (NHS). About 1 in 100 adults is already sensitized to PPD, the main dark-dye chemical, and 4.6% of dermatitis patients react to it.** NHS 2025; *Contact Dermatitis* 2015 and 2009 (21,515 patients). [DATA]
4. **In India, 42% of hair-dye users reported side effects, and 1 in 3 never do a skin test.** *Int J Trichology*, 2013. [DATA, peer-reviewed; small single-site sample]
5. **The global hair-colour market is worth $28 billion (2025), heading to $47 billion by 2034. US at-home dye alone passed $2.4 billion in 2020.** Fortune Business Insights 2026; Mintel via YPulse 2021. [MARKET REPORT]
6. **Nearly half (47.8%) of first-time home dyers feared the result wouldn't be what they wanted, and 42.7% feared picking the wrong shade.** Garnier/OnePoll, 2,000 US women, Jan 2021. [BRAND SURVEY: the market leader's own data]
7. **76% of UK women say their home colour never matches a salon result.** Wella, 1,000+ women, 2017. [BRAND SURVEY]
8. **About a third of UK women who do their own hair (34%) have had to pay a professional to fix it.** OnePoll, 2,000 women, May 2022. [BRAND SURVEY; covers cutting and colouring]
9. **A box costs under $20 for most people (73%), but the average colour-correction appointment costs $210, and up to $405 in California.** Better Natured 2024; StyleSeat booking data. [BRAND SURVEY + WEAK SOURCE; present as "about 10x the cost to fix"]
