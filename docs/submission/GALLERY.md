# Gallery images and captions

Devpost shows gallery images at 3:2. Make each image **1500 × 1000** (or any 3:2 size). Put a phone screenshot on a plain dark background, and add a short title in big text if it helps. The caption goes in Devpost's caption box under each image.

How to take the screenshots: open the app on your phone, use **Example shades** (no brand names or boxes in images), and take normal phone screenshots. Then place them on a 1500×1000 canvas in CapCut or Canva. Keep the app's dark theme, so all the images look like one set.

Order matters: the first image is also the thumbnail on the gallery page.

## Thumbnail (the gallery card picture)

Use image 1: the two results side by side on the same face, with "The box vs your hair" in large letters and the word "Bense" in a corner. It has to read at 330 pixels wide, so use big text and only two faces. No logos or brand boxes.

## Rules for every caption

- Say what the picture proves, in one or two normal sentences.
- No "seamless", "revolutionary", "AI-powered magic".
- If a photo is a sample photo, not you, say so: "sample photo from YouCam's documentation".
- If anything is sped up, say so.
