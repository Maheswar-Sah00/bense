## What the winners did, and how this pack copies it

Based on 27 winning entries from 20 hackathons (2024–2026), including all five YouCam edition 1 winners:

1. **Open with something concrete, not a wish.** Losers opened with "Skincare is often based on guesswork." Winners opened with a scene: "Two bottles. Same size, same cap, same weight." Ours: the same box, two results, on the same face, then your girlfriend's three days.
2. **Put the statistics early, cite them, then stop.** Winners used 0 to 12 numbers, all in the first section, with the source in brackets. Ours: six outside numbers, each with a source, then only our own measurements.
3. **Your own measured number beats a market number.** ShadeSpan's "Two out of fourteen" won 2nd place. Ours: "48 of 178".
4. **Headings that argue and match the judging criteria.** For example "How we used the YouCam API (Technological Implementation)" and "Honesty: what is measured, what is modelled".
5. **Show the integration, don't claim it.** Endpoints, the green-mask trick, seconds and units measured live.
6. **Say what you don't know.** Every 2026 YouCam winner had an honesty or limits section. Judges trust a project that tells them its weak spots.
7. **Personal story: specific, short, and no overclaim.** The best one (veTriage) said "I would never claim that AI saved that dog." Ours: "I can't promise it would have saved her those three days."
8. **Video: problem in the first 10 seconds, the product working on a real phone for most of it, the source on every number, chapters in the description, under 3 minutes.**
9. **Plain words.** The 2026 sponsor-API winners used none of: seamless, revolutionize, leverage, empower, cutting-edge.
